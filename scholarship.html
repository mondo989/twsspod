About the Event

A Social Like Never Before

You are invited to That's What She Said's Annual Scholarship Social! This is an evening dedicated to celebrating empowerment, elevating each other, and supporting education in the Great Lakes Bay Region.

Join us for an unforgettable evening featuring food, drinks, guest speakers, exclusive merch, and meaningful connections with inspiring women from our community.

All proceeds support scholarships for deserving young women pursuing higher education. Together, we're making a difference!
