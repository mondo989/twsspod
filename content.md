That’s What She Said” with Christiana Malacara is a captivating radio talk show dedicated to celebrating women’s empowerment. The show focuses on the incredible women from the Great Lakes Bay Region from all walks of life, diving into their personal and professional lives, reflecting, and creating connections.

Each episode features a detailed conversation with a guest, exploring who she is, what she does for a living, and the unique story of her journey. These discussions highlight the woman’s personal growth, the challenges she has overcome, and the accomplishments she has achieved. Through these inspiring stories, the show aims to shed light on the diverse paths to success and the power of resilience and determination. In addition to sharing their stories, the women on “That’s What She Said” discuss their approaches to self-improvement, and empowerment and how they inspire and support others.
