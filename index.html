About the Show

Empowering Women,
One Story at a Time

"That's What She Said" with Christiana Malacara is a captivating radio talk show dedicated to celebrating women's empowerment. The show focuses on the incredible women from the Great Lakes Bay Region from all walks of life, diving into their personal and professional lives, reflecting, and creating connections.

Each episode features a detailed conversation with a guest, exploring who she is, what she does for a living, and the unique story of her journey. These discussions highlight the woman's personal growth, the challenges she has overcome, and the accomplishments she has achieved.
